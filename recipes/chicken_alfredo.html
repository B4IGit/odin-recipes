Chicken Alfredo Recipe

Fire Roasted Chicken Alfredo

Indulge in the ultimate comfort food featuring juicy, fire-roasted chicken breast and tender fettuccine pasta, all coated in a rich, velvety garlic and Parmesan cream sauce.

This classic Italian-American dish promises a luxurious dining experience that will make any night feel special.

Ingredients

  • Olive oil
  • Boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • Kosher salt
  • Ground pepper
  • Garlic cloves
  • Low-sodium chicken broth
  • Whole milk
  • Fettuccine
  • Parmesan, finely grated
  • Heavy cream
  • Parsley, finely chopped (for serving)
